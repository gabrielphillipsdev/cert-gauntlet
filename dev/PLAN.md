### Engine extraction (the part nobody sees but everything depends on)

The audit confirmed Net-Study and Sec-Study are copy-pastes: Sec+ constants, domain loops, storage keys (`secplus-`), PBQ types and generators are baked into `index.html`/`engine.js`. The engine becomes cert-agnostic: a pack declares its domains and weights, its exam rules (count, time, backtracking, PBQ placement, pass score), and which PBQ/sim types it uses. Card leveling, spaced repetition, twins, speed rounds, missed→drill, Sure/Leaning/No-clue, readiness estimator, backup/restore all carry over unchanged.

### Sync — the thing that will bite you on two devices if we don't fix it

Today's sync is whole-file last-writer-wins with no re-pull after launch: open on iPhone and iPad at once and the second save silently wipes the first. In-progress exams don't sync at all. New design, still on your free secret Gist (no server, no cost):

- **Per-record merge**, not whole-file: every card stat, exam result, and sim result carries its own `updatedAt`; merge takes the newer record, unions exam history by id. Two devices can both study the same day without loss.
- **Pull on launch and on every return to the app** (visibility/focus), push debounced after changes, retry when back online.
- **In-progress exam synced** as its own file — start an exam on iPad, finish on iPhone.
- **Offline**: service worker caches the app shell and packs; versioned so updates land cleanly. Studies on the plane; syncs when back.
- Token stays a fine-grained PAT with gist scope only. Migration imports your existing `secplus-state.json` on first launch so no progress is lost.

## 3. Security+ pack — fix and finish, built to the real PBQ format

**Audit verdict:** content is good (all three 85-question banks + 398 cards pass validation, domain weighting matches 701 exactly, ~90 items spot-checked with no wrong keys). Exam mode is close to real. **PBQs are the gap** — everything is tap-to-pair or type-a-number, and the real exam uses simulations: CompTIA's own words are "approximate environments or tools — firewalls, network diagrams, terminals, operating systems — with restricted functionality but multiple solution paths," with a per-item Reset, partial credit, and revisiting allowed. Your roommate's PBQ (console → list services → compare to IoC list → stop services → block IPs) is exactly that style.

### Content fixes carried into the new pack

- Pass mark 720 → **750**; show raw % alongside scaled so you know what 750 feels like (community consensus: low-to-mid 80s raw).
- Two "order" PBQs grade an arbitrary strict sequence (order of volatility: RAM vs network state are the same RFC 3227 tier; change-management flow has no canonical order in 701) → accept equivalent orders.
- Malware cards are filed under the social-engineering category → move to `attacks` (fixes distractor pools too).
- Remove the exam-mode Pause button (real exam has none); keep it in practice mode only.
- Every item tagged with its 701 objective ID (e.g. `2.4`) so an 801 re-map later is a content job, not a rebuild.
